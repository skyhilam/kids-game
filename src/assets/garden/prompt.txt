Use case: stylized-concept. One production transparent PNG atlas of sixteen garden sprites for a preschool vocabulary game.
Image 1 is SUBJECT reference ONLY: a photographed garden vocabulary page. Use its plants, animals and watering can concepts only, not its printed illustrations, book, layout, mascot, lettering or photographed setting. Image 2 is PROJECT STYLE AND SPACING: soft warm gouache, delicate cocoa outlines, gently dimensional shading, rounded forms, wide empty transparent gutters.
Exactly four equal columns x four equal rows on a square canvas. One complete isolated subject per cell, centered, each within central 60% of both cell width and height.
